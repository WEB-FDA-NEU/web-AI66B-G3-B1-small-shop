
# Project Title

Management & Business Operations

# Selected Topic

Small Business Sales Management System

# Planned Features
1 . Product & Inventory management
  - Manage product information and track stock levels.
  - Automatically update inventory when a sale is completed.

2 . Sales order management
  - Create and manage sales orders.
  - Handle order details and product quantities.

3 . Revenue and sales analytics
  - View daily revenue reports.
  - Display top-selling products based on sales data.

4 . Roles and permissions
  - Support different access levels for different roles.
  - Control available features based on choosen roles
